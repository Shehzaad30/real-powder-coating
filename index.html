How it's done

Four stages. No shortcuts.

Powder coat is only as good as the prep beneath it. Each part moves through every stage in order — that's why our finish holds.

STAGE 01

Strip & blast

Old coatings, rust and mill scale are media-blasted back to clean, profiled bare metal.

STAGE 02

Pretreat

A chemical wash and phosphate treatment etch the surface and lock out corrosion under the coat.

STAGE 03

Electrostatic spray

Charged powder is applied to grounded parts for an even, wrap-around coat with no runs or drips.

STAGE 04

Oven cure

Parts bake at 180–200°C so the powder flows and cross-links into one continuous, impact-hard shell.
